01 · Instant App Clip & Roster

Send one link. Everyone’s in.

Drop a destination and share a single link in iMessage. Friends tap to join via an iOS App Clip in under two seconds—no App Store download or account creation required. See who is En Route, who is Pending, and send a one-tap reminder right from the live sheet.

02 · 3D Multi-Map & Core ML ETAs

Every route. Synchronized.

Watch everyone’s real-time street polyline, live distance, and turn-by-turn guidance on a shared 2D or 3D MapKit canvas. An on-device Core ML arrival model continuously blends speed, route geometry, and traffic to keep every participant’s countdown accurate to the second.

03 · Live Activities, Dynamic Island & CarPlay

Glanceable from Lock Screen to dashboard.

Never text while driving. ActivityKit pins a live joint progress bar to your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, while CarPlay puts group ETAs on your dashboard—and automatically ends the session the moment everyone arrives.

Whatever shape your meetup takes.

Group dinners. Stop holding a table alone while appetizers get cold. Share one link in iMessage and see who is en route, who is three minutes out, and who still needs a quick one-tap reminder.

Road trip convoys. Keep every car in the group visible in 3D MapKit and CarPlay. See each driver’s live road polyline, exact distance, and turn-by-turn arrival time without making a single phone call.

Curbside pickups. No more circling airport loops or getting waved off by traffic officers. Follow the Live Joint Navigation progress bar right on your iPhone Lock Screen and pull up to the curb at the exact minute.

Client visits and teams. Coordinate on-site meetups with an instant invite link that tracks accepted and pending attendees in one place—and automatically stops sharing the moment everyone arrives.
