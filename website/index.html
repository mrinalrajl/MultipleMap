01 · Instant App Clip & RSVP Roster

Send one link. Everyone’s in.

Drop a destination and share a single invite link in iMessage. Friends tap to open the native iOS App Clip in under two seconds—no App Store download or sign-up required. Track En Route, Pending, and Declined status chips in the glass drawer and send one-tap SMS reminders.

02 · 2D/3D Multi-Map & Turn-by-Turn HUD

Every route. Synchronized.

See every accepted friend’s color-coded MapKit street polyline, live road distance badge, and next turn maneuver on one unified 2D or 62°-pitch 3D camera—with a one-tap handoff to Apple Maps.

Glanceable from Lock Screen to dashboard.

ActivityKit pins a live joint progress bar to your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, while the native CarPlay dashboard puts attendee distances, live routes, and arrival times right on your vehicle’s screen.
