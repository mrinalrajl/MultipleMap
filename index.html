01 · Instant Link & Live Guest List

Send one link. Everyone’s in.

Pick a meeting spot and share a single link in iMessage. Friends tap to join in two seconds—no app download or sign-up needed. See who is En Route, who is Pending, and send a quick one-tap reminder right from the list.

02 · Live 3D Group Map

Every route. One screen.

Watch every friend’s real street route, live distance badge, and next turn on one shared 2D or 3D map—with a one-tap button to jump straight into Apple Maps.

03 · Lock Screen, Dynamic Island & CarPlay

Glanceable from Lock Screen to dashboard.

Check everyone’s live arrival progress right on your iPhone Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, or on your car’s CarPlay screen without ever picking up your phone while driving.

Whatever shape your meetup takes.

Group dinners. Stop holding a table alone while appetizers get cold. Share one link in iMessage and see who is on the way, who is three minutes out, and who still needs a quick one-tap reminder.

Road trip convoys. Keep every car in the group visible in 3D and on CarPlay. See each driver’s live road route, exact distance, and arrival time without making a single phone call.

Curbside pickups. No more circling airport loops or getting waved off by traffic officers. Follow the live arrival bar right on your iPhone Lock Screen and pull up to the curb at the exact minute.

Client visits and teams. Coordinate on-site meetups with an instant invite link that tracks who accepted and who’s pending in one place—and automatically stops sharing the moment everyone arrives.
